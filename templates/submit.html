How Placement Works

When you click Submit, the Flask backend validates the incident and invokes priority_queue.add_request().

  • Priority 1 (Critical) jumps directly ahead of Priority 2, 3, and 4.
  • If there are other Priority 1 emergencies already waiting, arrival order is strictly maintained (FIFO).
  • The record is saved to MySQL table emergency_requests with status 'Pending'.

Viva Note

Professors will ask why Priority 1 is at index 0. You can explain that lower numerical integers represent higher urgency levels, minimizing human sorting errors.
