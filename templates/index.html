Why a Priority Queue instead of standard FIFO?

In normal queues (First-In, First-Out), requests are processed strictly by arrival time. However, in emergency dispatch, a life-threatening Accident (Priority 1) arriving at 10:05 AM must NOT wait behind a minor sprain (Priority 4) that arrived at 10:00 AM.

RAPIDREQ uses an explicit Priority Queue where the highest urgency (lowest numerical priority value: 1 = Critical) is always dispatched first. When priorities are tied, FIFO order is preserved to ensure fair processing.
